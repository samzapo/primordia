# Revision75 — twelve living cells per family

Owner-directed main hotfix, October19,2026. Each lineage keeps at most12 living
cells. A normal paid division still creates and registers its daughter, awards
parent1AP and daughter1AP and consumes its ordinary division resources. If this
puts the family over12, that daughter immediately merges into its actual parent
before follow selection can switch to the consumed body.

Existing oversized families reconcile during ordinary authoritative ticking,
including the resident ecology path. Youngest generations donate first,
preferring their surviving older parent; otherwise use an older relative.
Within one generation, greater age then lower cell ID selects the elder.
Connected selected cells cannot donate; if protected donors prevent reduction,
retain the remaining bodies and retry later. This is the narrow cap exception
to cue/contact/age/cooldown requirements, not a change to normal merge sensing.

Use the existing finite merge transaction, organ reabsorption/refunds,90%
material/ATP yield, waste/heat, all remaining AP, internal food/gases and bounded
pull-in illustration. Retain exactly-once removal, lineage lifetime/birth
counters, consumed-body retirement without death reports, same pond, accounts,
five keys and feedback. No biomass/AP ceiling, reset, destructive migration or
new culling mechanism. Startup checkpoints still compare all saved cells before
first tick; cap reconciliation then changes the live population intentionally.
Read-only health includes the limit, largest family and over-limit-family count.

The limit and nonlocal forced fusion are game rules, not biological claims.
Behavior and investment continue unchanged on the surviving anatomy; no organ
capability is added. Update the player guide and append one stable-only notice.
Run finite paid-birth/merge/accounting, ownership, saved-world retention and
required functional GPU checks; rendering-speed benchmarks remain held.

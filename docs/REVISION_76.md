# Revision76 — experimental lifecycle and biomass gameplay
Owner approved October19,2026. Experimental only; main remains unchanged.

Construction uses biomass, with two reusable free non-receptor places per cell. The oral groove is optional (8 biomass outside the allowance), can be reabsorbed, and owns its mandatory full-tension opposite-wall tether. Starter leaf+groove use the allowance. Free material gives no reabsorption refund. Dedicated sensing receptors remain free and excluded. AP is retired without conversion.

Mitosis uses half available biomass for a30-body daughter and her remaining biomass, plus two free starter organs. It pays20ATP and divides remaining ATP equally. Minimum84biomass/56ATP. Budding spends42biomass and60ATP, leaving8biomass/18ATP in the parent (minimum50biomass/78ATP). Daughters start with a leaf and optional groove. Internal contents partition1/2 or1/4; no parent organs are cloned. Both automatic modes start on; each parent/newborn has a saved60-second cooldown. No division investment or clear-water gate. Cap births immediately reabsorb the excess daughter before body registration. Random below-cap fusion is removed.

A favorite cell is the account's last directly controlled selection, with a first name and gold outline. Offline/paused/hidden accounts protect it from death, organelle loss and automatic absorption; resources still deplete. Active sessions restore vulnerability. Session presence is authenticated.

Custom investment snapshots a relative's anatomy and falls back deterministically to10/45/45Hunter/Producer/Grazer after matching it. One automatic action/minute, Manual default.

No delayed reserve-food regeneration loop. Crowding checks recent actual family signals; >6for10seconds permits a five-second probability min(1%,0.2%per excess), once/family/minute, with three-second lysis warning. Organic mass above3000 causes recoverable leakage; protection adds1000capacity/healthy standard organ,1%excess/second until90%capacity. Protected favorites do not autolyse or suffer stress loss.

Remove scripted three-crisis gameplay, rings, goals, tides and endings; keep persistent mutations, lineage generation/totals and hourly environmental weather. Gameplay/Mechanics and one experimental-only consolidated announcement reflect this revision.

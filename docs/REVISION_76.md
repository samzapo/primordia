# Revision76 — experimental lifecycle and biomass gameplay
Owner approved October19,2026. Experimental only; main remains unchanged.

Starter leaf+groove use the allowance. Free material gives no reabsorption refund. Dedicated sensing receptors remain free and excluded. AP is retired without conversion.

Mitosis uses half available biomass for a30-body daughter and her remaining biomass, plus two free starter organs. It pays20ATP and divides remaining ATP equally. Minimum84biomass/56ATP. Cap births immediately reabsorb the excess daughter before body registration. Random below-cap fusion is removed.

A favorite cell is the account's last directly controlled selection, with a first name and gold outline. Offline/paused/hidden accounts protect it from death, organelle loss and automatic absorption; resources still deplete. Active sessions restore vulnerability. Session presence is authenticated.

No delayed reserve-food regeneration loop. Organic mass above3000 causes recoverable leakage; protection adds1000capacity/healthy standard organ,1%excess/second until90%capacity. Protected favorites do not autolyse or suffer stress loss.

Merge artwork has an independent live presentation clock, and is transient, nonphysical and bounded. Tombstones prevent buffered resurrection. Cell-only damping changes body response; particle transport/collisions/diffusion and global medium viscosity remain unchanged.

Remove scripted three-crisis gameplay, rings, goals, tides and endings; keep persistent mutations, lineage generation/totals and hourly environmental weather. Gameplay/Mechanics and one experimental-only consolidated announcement reflect this revision.

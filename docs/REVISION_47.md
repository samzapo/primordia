# Revision 47 — automatic paid reproduction

Living cells now invest surplus tissue and actual ATP particles into their existing offspring bud. This runs after paid physiology in detailed and coarse execution, without relying on a connected client or camera. The routine runs every half second, transfers at most ten units per simulated second, retains twelve usable tissue and twenty operating ATP, and stops at the current anatomy quote and sixty reserved ATP. These policy defaults are untuned; no survival or gameplay-balance result is claimed.

A funded bud attempts ordinary paid division automatically. The parent remains selected; the child becomes a living family member. The same anatomy, compartment/chemical partition, safe-water placement, four-member living-family limit and finite material/ATP costs apply to manual and automatic release. A blocked birth retains its inventory and retries no more than twice per simulated second. A shared 64-live-cell bound respects the resident exchange capacity. Newborns wait for the next simulation iteration before reproducing. Dormant bodies remain dormant until needed for death recovery or an explicit action; no reserve is farmed while asleep.

The policy applies to unowned cells as well as account lineages. An unowned family gets a ledger when it first divides; joining an existing unclaimed family reuses its ledger and descendants. Reproduction does not borrow the managed player's anatomy, lineage, resources or evolution context. Shared editors continue using live paid commands without pausing the world. Local paused editing retains its existing protection.

Death recovery was already automatic: select an existing living relative, otherwise hatch an already-paid dormant reserve when safe placement is available. A lineage with neither is extinct. Persistent simulation never fabricates a new founder, resources or a new pond merely because a player signs in. Snapshots now report recovery state so the mobile view distinguishes a waiting reserve from an exhausted lineage. Family history, world time and access keys are preserved.

The biological division and dormancy abstractions remain those described in Revision 16 and references.md. This revision adds automation policy, not a new chemical mechanism. Actual ATP-to-reserve transfers retain particle positions and finite fuel; resource totals are readouts. Existing prepared reserves are checked even when there is no surplus to invest.

Gameplay/objective/balance tests remain held. Keep finite transfer, paid division, ownership, bounded retry, checkpoint, network and real-GPU infrastructure checks. Do not run survival or population-tuning campaigns to validate this revision.

## Shared cell instincts and investment

Owner continuation, October17: replace manual Exploration/response cycles and per-organ Steering percentages with a shared behavior tree; continually maintain both this tree and investment strategies as organs are added. Autonomous exploration remains a fallback action, not the removed player-selected percentage/duty cycle. This is an ordinary development rule, not a scheduled autonomous task.

The priority selector in cell_behavior.gd receives only the cell's own reserves/injury, finite food queries, local illumination/acidity and immutable received pheromone reports. It never receives live neighboring bodies, player selection, strategy names, camera visibility or a remote optimum. Every cell uses this selector, including selected players, unattended owned relatives, NPCs, fine, resident and coarse biology. Their real organs determine capability; all chemistry, movement, attacks and purchases retain the ordinary material/ATP/AP/contact rules.

Priority order:
1. A working flight receptor sensing harmful acid chooses the local downhill gradient.
2. A recent physical hit triggers a three-second escape reflex through existing movement/sensing anatomy. Flight receptors also respond to locally reported threats. Escape directions separate from the nearest locally reported relative, blended so separation cannot turn back into the threat.
3. Low ATP favors available food, useful light, then a feasible hunt; if actual food is already inside, absence of a better local cue permits resting while digestion continues.
4. Low biomass favors accessible food, then eligible prey, then light.
5. Healthy cells follow eligible prey, collect food if reserves are not full, seek their leaves' preferred light, or search smoothly when no useful cue is received.

ATP urgency enters below18 times growth scale and clears above36; low biomass enters below max(4,10% body) and clears above max(8,22% body). These are authored initial playtest values. Signal delivery still travels through the cell, memory remains at most1.2seconds/actual pheromone expiry, emergencies interrupt lower priorities, and within a priority the existing20% switch margin avoids near-tie flicker. Smooth deterministic search uses cell age/id/pond seed without consuming ecological RNG or knowing remote food. Rest only stops active propulsion: passive fluid coupling, upkeep, pumping and finite digestion continue. No instant healing, kills, food or ATP are created.

Family perception uses a separate bounded chemical query at the first working social receptor. Resident receipts/summaries and cold/native/reference paths agree on the nearest fresh emission-time report. Own/family reports never become hunt or flight targets. No extra alarm chemical or live family-location API is added.

Old exploration/response_cycle/organ.steering/output fields remain unchanged in saved bodies and offspring for exact retention, but are inert. Old editor commands are rejected. A bounded saved behavior dictionary keeps resource latches, a recent injury direction/deadline and the delivered escape heading. Nucleus → Behavior describes current instincts; no permanent HUD, controls or screen labels are added.

Investment remains opt-in for player families and manual for a connected selected cell. Global NPC assignment remains floor(10%)Hunter, with the remainder evenly Producer/Grazer. Each role has two deterministic supporting-anatomy preferences, working-organ caps and diminishing repetition. Hunters acquire a receptor, a weapon and movement; Producers acquire leaves then support; Grazers acquire ciliary feeding currents and digestion/recycling support. Missing support, damage/acid stress and actual oxygen change purchase priorities. Newly purchased photosynthetic leaves choose Sun/Shade from local light; existing leaves are never rewritten. The acid-assisted Grazer builds buffer/repair support before acid secretion. All purchases use the same AP price, fitting, external-input accounting and saved family ledger; proposals consume no resources or RNG.

Every future organ/mechanic change must explicitly review: its cue/capability in the tree, its role (or intentional manual-only status) in investment, physiological costs and physical effects, player guide/release text, and targeted behavior/strategy tests. Preserve one tree and shared rules rather than special NPC/player controllers.

# Revision 51 — division rewards, bounded metabolism and mobile controls

## Division and mutations
Every committed paid birth, including formation of a prepaid dormant body, awards
one AP to that lineage. Failed placement/funding, dormant hatching, following,
login and restored history award nothing. The family ledger and account share
one checkpointed progression object; rewards happen inside the committed birth
rather than by rescanning old events. Unowned lineages keep their progression
when adopted by an account.

Each successful division independently has a ten percent chance to offer one
unlearned mutation. Offers persist until accepted at the nucleus; choosing and
applying a known mutation cost neither AP nor biomass. An organ accepts one
matching specialization. Free traits change the organ's parameters without
fabricating extra material. The nucleus marker and birth notification announce
available offers. Existing ordinary organ construction and restoration prices
continue to apply. No legacy tide objectives are reinstated in persistent mode.

## Calendar conditions
The authority samples one world-wide condition per UTC calendar hour, using the
pond seed and hour/date. A stylized time-of-day/season light level is modulated
by clear/hazy/cloudy/overcast weather; the driven-current target also changes
direction and strength. Existing finite-mass flow responds gradually to the new
target. No field or particle pool is reset and no food/acid is introduced.
Conditions stay fixed within an hour and survive restart without rerolling.
Browser snapshots carry the same conditions for all accounts. This is fictional
pond weather, not an astronomical or geographically calibrated model.

## Small intracellular chemistry loop
Retain at least ten seconds of food-vacuole residence, one moving carrier for
food/waste contents, and numeric ATP, biomass and offspring ATP reserves. Add
only finite oxygen and carbon parcels, with at most six of each per cell;
food/waste keep twelve rows each. Total stock rows are bounded to 36 in a
64-slot resident allocation. There are no seeded cofactor clouds or pathway
editors. Onscreen authority stays on the resident GPU; hidden chemistry uses
the existing retained-time coarse path.

Digestion consumes oxygen within 20 world units of a food carrier. Oxygenated
material retains up to 0.7 tissue and yields up to 2.25 numeric ATP per fueled
food unit; fermentation retains 0.3 tissue and yields up to 1 ATP. Both retain
0.1 carbon material, with remaining organic material in waste. All credited
work and retained tissue are bounded by actual food fuel; unused fuel is
recorded as heat. These game ratios are not molecular stoichiometry.

Photosynthetic organs use local carbon within 24 units and actual light.
Carbon becomes 0.35 biomass and 0.65 fueled food, with numeric ATP and oxygen
production. Solar energy input is recorded explicitly. Carbon/oxygen move as
small shaped particles and cross only at the actual membrane; waste leaves
only through a waste-filled carrier at the cytoproct. Local oxygen/carbon
storage limits apply to external uptake (carbon capacity is restored to 30 per growth scale). Gas releases retain sub-0.3 remainders inside their physical parcels until enough material accumulates; this bounds tiny emissions without discarding material. Existing light seeking remains.

With ATP below 10 and food below 1, surplus biomass above a protected floor of
8 can become finite food fuel at the existing bounded mobilization rate.
Nothing is credited without consuming that biomass.

CPU and GPU share finite quantities/fuel and reaction rules. Native feeding
reads actual particle totals after each uptake rather than writing scalar gas
stores back over particle authority. Checkpoints retain particle species,
identities, ages, RNG, account progression and pending mutation offers.
The authority archives a pre-upgrade saved pond before the first new-model
checkpoint; rollback needs that archive as well as the old executable.

## Presentation
All secretion-capable organs bounce on actual successful particle production,
using a presentation-only damped animation keyed by a secretion serial.
Photosynthesis triggers the same feedback. Animation never spends resources,
draws simulation randomness or emits particles.

Interoception restores the paired pulsing contractile-vacuole/radial-canal art.
It remains visual-only and does not add osmotic physiology. ATP spending/gain
and repair feedback use small shaped marks; ATP has a yellow square-root-shaped
glyph. These marks illustrate numerical transactions rather than introducing
physical ATP stocks. Food/waste illustrations remain attached to their single
carrier and gases share the native/browser shaped-particle inspection path.

Microtubules remain seeded/inherited structural anatomy. Remove player editing
and reject its old network commands. Render only faint links, without junction
handles, anchor dots or selection highlights.

## Touch interface
Keep the selected organ's circular icon at the upper left throughout placement
or movement; tapping it cancels the held action. All mobile hover/expanded
descriptions and notifications use a bounded three-line bottom text strip
above the hideable resource display. Particle descriptions use the same bottom
region. Comic-style warning typography remains readable.

The existing mobile Pause button freezes the client's view and opens settings.
Resume closes settings and returns to current authority state. The shared pond
continues throughout. Desktop M/settings, I/perception and ordinary keyboard
view Pause remain available. Existing compact music/Follow utilities retain
their dimensions.

## Validation
Gameplay, objectives and balance remain enabled. Validate division exactly-once
rewards/free mutations, hourly/restart consistency, material/fuel conservation,
local reactions and gas exchange, native/reference/GPU agreement, mobile
actions, actual pixel rendering, saved-world recovery and real-time frame/tick
budgets. Functional workstation rendering does not establish physical phone
frame rate or device acceptance.

Decorative AP parcels are capped at sixteen representative marks; numerical AP remains exact. Desktop comparison layout is bounded as AP accumulates across long-lived lineages.

Membrane/cytoproct release uses bounded elapsed-rate credits and up to 1.8 units per finite parcel, matching the existing external resolution. Sub-0.3 gas remainders stay inside; waste vacuoles release their complete remaining stock when capacity permits. Capacity credits carry no chemical material and survive checkpoints/ownership changes. There is no exterior merge shortcut or pose teleport to meet the density target.

Mobile settings use device-density-scaled text and touch targets, start below the Pause/zoom row and temporarily hide duplicate utility controls. Browser Resume explicitly closes settings before applying its buffered snapshot. The view's presentation clock freezes with local Pause. Mobile instructions describe taps and the held-icon cancel action; desktop keyboard/mouse hints remain available.


## Membrane contour artifact repair
A reversed neighboring pair in a transient contour previously made the positive
angle wrap take almost a complete revolution. Its four spline samples formed a
box-shaped inner membrane lip. Native and browser outline generation now validate
a single ordered revolution; malformed input uses the physics solver's bounded
polar sectors for presentation while preserving radii. Healthy outlines retain
their existing geometry. This does not modify saved physical nodes, resources,
collision rules or simulation timing. Raster regressions cover the ordinary
contour and reversed pairs on both sides of the signed-angle seam.

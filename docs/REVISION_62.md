# Revision 62 — quarter-capacity low-power organs

Owner direction, October 17, 2026: underpowered organs should work at one-quarter capacity until powered again, rather than disabling.

Cell.work_capacity is a read-only shared rule. Operating state enters below 12 ATP and returns to full at 18 ATP, with a bounded behavior.low_power latch to prevent rapid switching. A particular operation whose full cost exceeds available ATP also uses quarter capacity. Calls that perform work update the latch; status, UI and coarse collection requests only read it. Existing checkpoints need no anatomy or resource migration.

Movement impulse and ciliary/oral pumping use 25% of normal strength and requested operating ATP. Gland secretion schedules one-quarter of the ordinary finite-packet rate. Weapons retain their finite full burst and physical pore-assembly packets, but their cooldown clock advances at 25% speed, giving four times longer recharge. Active repair/cleaning use quarter rates; repair still consumes actual tissue, and ordinary passive hole closure/unbinding remain. A working repair gland can continue at zero ATP, without granting free biomass. Destroyed organs, empty material, absent target signals and idle secretion remain real limits.

Spend only ATP that actually exists; budget records never pretend the residual work was funded or create ATP. This 25% residual baseline is an explicit gameplay allowance, not a microscopic energy model. Food, oxygen and carbon processing remain finite. Photosynthesis, respiration/digestion, passive sensing and cortex protection keep their existing ATP-independent operation so recovery is possible. Starvation, death and reproductive/build budgets are unchanged.

Fine, native/resident orchestration and offscreen biology call the same work helpers. Offscreen swept feeding includes the same reduced pump component while preserving passive collection/current. Hunting intent may follow a usable local prey trace at low ATP when food/light are unavailable, while stored food still favors resting and emergency escape remains first. All investment profiles favor a first mitochondrion during shortage only with existing food and oxygen; roles, quotas/inheritance and normal AP/fitting remain.

Readiness labels say 25% capacity rather than paused and show actual slower weapon recharge. No new permanent HUD is added. Release notices remain experimental-only, and ordinary rollout preserves the current pond and saved cell identities. Validate actual activity/recovery, material and ATP accounting, same-seed reference/native/GPU behavior and saved-world restart without rendering-speed benchmarks.
